Sum up 25 November's world news in one image: this program draws a 1024×1024 poster of Wednesday 25 November 2026 — NATO sends early-warning AWACS aircraft to Poland after Russian missiles and drones crossed allied airspace; Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, a Politburo member once tipped for promotion, is placed under investigation for suspected serious violations of discipline and law; a Brooklyn jury convicts Huawei on 10 of 12 counts, including bank fraud, sanctions violations and stealing US trade secrets, while clearing it of racketeering; gunmen hold 25 people hostage in a Cartagena supermarket after a failed robbery, freeing five elderly women as police surround the store; Israel's defence minister says Iran is developing chemical weapons; and the United Nations reports that partners or relatives killed five women every hour in 2025. Written in Node 22 with the standard library only; the PNG, its bitmap font and every icon are drawn by hand in `main.ts`, so each run writes byte-identical output.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
